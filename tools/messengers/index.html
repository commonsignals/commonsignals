    AI messenger map.

    A directory of people and organisations explaining AI risk to the public, across both the existential-risk and present-day-harms conversations.

    Listing here is not an endorsement. These bios describe what each person or organisation does; Common Signals does not rate, rank or assess them.

    Suggest a messenger.

    We're looking for people and organisations who: speak to a public audience (not just other researchers or industry insiders), publish roughly monthly (or more often) on AI risk, have verifiable links (a website or social profile we can check). We don't list lab communications accounts, vendors selling AI products or services, or academics who only publish for other researchers.

    United States

    Berkeley nonprofit founded in 2000 that argues building superintelligence with current methods would most likely cause human extinction. Its president Nate Soares co-wrote the 2025 book If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies and calls for a global moratorium on superintelligence development.
