    What this can and can't tell you. Comment threads are self-selected and unrepresentative of any population. Engagement measures salience, not persuasion. One artefact tests one message, one messenger and one format at once, so nothing here separates them. Treat every finding as a hypothesis for a segmented message test, which is what our research programme exists to run.
