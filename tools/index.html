AI messenger map.

A directory of people and organisations explaining AI risk to the public, across both the existential-risk and present-day-harms conversations. Searchable and filterable by focus, type, region and channel.

Open the AI messenger map →

AI attitudes segment calculator.

A working demo of a segmentation calculator: answer ten questions and see where you land. The segments are illustrative placeholders, not the output of our pilot study.

Concept demo

Try the segment calculator →

Comment analyser.

Paste a Hacker News link or upload a comment export from any platform, and get a coded read of how a post landed: stance, frames, what carried the engagement, and the charts to show it. The same method as our published analyses, run on your thread.
