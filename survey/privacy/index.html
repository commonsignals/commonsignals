Privacy notice for this survey

This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your answers if you take part in this survey.

Who is running this

Common Signals, an independent, non-partisan research organisation. Questions can be sent to hello@commonsignals.org.

What we collect

Your answers to the survey questions, the order some questions were shown in, roughly how long you spent on each screen, and whether you completed the survey. If you arrived from Prolific, we also record your Prolific ID and session ID, used only to arrange payment.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address or any other directly identifying information unless you choose to write it into an open-text answer.

What happens to your Prolific ID

It is used only to arrange payment and to prevent duplicate entries while the study is open. Once a study closes, Prolific IDs are permanently removed from our database.

Publishing results

Anonymised, aggregated results are published openly at commonsignals.org/data so that researchers, journalists and the public can use them. No individual can be identified from the published data.

Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the browser window. Because responses are anonymous, we cannot retrieve or delete an individual response once submitted, as we have no way to identify which one is yours.

Contact

Questions about this survey or how your data is used can be sent to hello@commonsignals.org.
