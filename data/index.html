Data

Every coded comment behind our reception studies, browsable and filterable, comment by comment. The same data the articles are built from, published under CC BY 4.0.

Loading studies…

Each study is coded on stance, frame, format reaction and emotion against its own codebook, plus a handful of flags for what a comment mentions. Stance and emotion use mostly the same categories across the five studies; frame and format reaction are study-specific. Comment text is verbatim; author identities are hashed, not published. Full contract, JSON schemas and licensing: /data/studies/README.md. The coding and compilation are CC BY 4.0; comment text remains its authors'.
