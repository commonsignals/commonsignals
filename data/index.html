    Instagram comments on Nate Soares's reel "We stop AI. You help?"

    Instagram

    Coded2,136
    Positioned1,271
    Explore comments Read the article

    study.json·codebook·comments.csv

    Changelog (version 2)
    • v1, 29 Aug 2026: Logged-in API capture, 1,666 of 1,703 comments, coded with like counts available. Article published from it.
    • v2, 17 Sept 2026: Page-text recapture (2,136 of 2,715 comments then live; likely under-counts unexpanded reply threads, the same limitation seen in the METR Reddit capture) recoded against an 18-frame list. No like counts available in this capture, so engagement-weighted findings from v1 cannot be reproduced or updated. Supersedes v1 for stance/frame shares only.

    Each study is coded on stance, frame, format reaction and emotion against its own codebook, plus a handful of flags for what a comment mentions. Stance and emotion use mostly the same categories across studies; frame and format reaction are study-specific. Comment text is verbatim; author identities are hashed, not published. Full contract, JSON schemas and licensing: /data/studies/README.md. The coding and compilation are CC BY 4.0; comment text remains its authors'.
