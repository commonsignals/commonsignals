    Codebook: Facebook comments on a 21-minute Diary of a CEO cut of the AI debate

    Central claim

    Advanced AI could become impossible for humans to control and poses a serious risk, up to human extinction, that justifies slowing or stopping development.

    Stance note

    agree = accepts the risk case, sides with Soares or Yampolskiy, or attacks McAfee; disagree = rejects it (AI is a tool, hype or fearmongering), sides with McAfee, or attacks Soares or Yampolskiy; mixed = explicitly holds both positions.

    Stance Shared across the series

    Agree
    accepts the risk case, or sides with Soares or Yampolskiy, or attacks McAfee
    Disagree
    rejects the risk case, or sides with McAfee, or attacks Soares or Yampolskiy
    Mixed
    explicitly holds both positions
    Unclear
    about AI or the debate, but the position cannot be read
    Na
    does not engage with AI or AI risk (format, host, adverts, tags, emoji, off topic)

    Frame Specific to this study

    General agreement
    Agrees with the risk case without giving a further reasone.g. "This really is the whole argument in one sentence."
    Debater verdict
    Judges the panellists (who won, who was rude, who talked too much) more than the argumente.g. "Pretty sure AI is already smarter than the bald guy"
    Fatalism
    Doom, too late, resignation, grief for the futuree.g. "We're all doomed...there's no other way of putting it."
    Scifi
    Reads the risk through a film, TV or book storye.g. "Strong 'Don't Look Up' vibes with this interview."
    Blame people
    The danger is the humans who control or misuse AI (greed, power, bad actors), not AI itselfe.g. "the main risk is people with such enormous power going rogue"
    Human mirror
    AI is dangerous because it is trained on and copies human naturee.g. "you design an artificial mind based on humans and it acts like humans"
    Capability scepticism
    AI is just a tool or LLM, it is hype, the fear is scaremongeringe.g. "AI is so dumb. What are you talking about"
    Unplug
    We can switch it off, cut power or destroy data centres, or rebuttals that we cannote.g. "Can't we just unplug it ?"
    Takeover scenarios
    Speculation about how AI would harm or outwit us: hiding, replicating, sabotage, competing for resourcese.g. "We won't be seen as cattle more like weeds"
    Policy leaders
    Regulation, slowing down, prosecuting executives, political leaders, international racee.g. "Roman should speak to congress"
    Present harms
    Harms happening now: jobs, education, dependence, misuse todaye.g. "we stopped paying attention to what's happening now"
    Benefits
    AI's upside, or that the risk is worth takinge.g. "I would love to use AI to discover a cure"
    Religion spiritual
    Religious or spiritual readinge.g. "No one here wants to consider Revelation ch 13?"
    Conspiracy other target
    Conspiracy theories aimed at groups or institutionse.g. "Scare mongering, just like covid, fals flag incoming"
    Format only
    About the podcast itself: format, adverts, guests requestede.g. "Thumbs down for all these advertisement breaks."
    Other
    Fits none of the above

    Format reaction Shared across the series

    Praise
    praises the debate or clip itself
    Mock
    mocks the debate or clip
    Condescended
    feels talked down to by the debate or clip
    Imitates
    imitates or parodies the delivery
    None
    no reaction to the delivery
