    Codebook: YouTube comments on the Amanpour and Company Khlaaf segment

    Central claim

    The real story of the OpenAI agent-swarm incident is corporate negligence and hype, not a machine going rogue.

    Stance note

    agree = accepts Khlaaf's reframing that the real story is corporate negligence, not a rogue machine; disagree = rejects this and defends the rogue/dangerous-AI framing, including from the AI-safety side; mixed = accepts part, rejects or reframes part.

    Stance Shared across the series

    Agree
    accepts Khlaaf's reframing: corporate negligence, not a rogue machine
    Disagree
    rejects this; defends the rogue/dangerous-AI framing
    Mixed
    accepts part, rejects or reframes part
    Na
    on topic but no readable position
    Unclear
    genuinely cannot be told either way; used sparingly

    Frame Specific to this study

    Accountability frame
    the real problem is corporate negligence, decisions or incentives, not the AI acting on its own
    Warning-shot dispute
    disputes Khlaaf's account from the AI-safety side; argues she understates what the swarm did
    Hype or staged
    dismisses the incident or segment as staged, exaggerated or a stunt, without a specific capability argument
    Capability scepticism
    doubts the premise on technical grounds; the incident is overblown because the AI isn't that capable
    Credential attack
    attacks Khlaaf's expertise or trustworthiness, naming her OpenAI background or credentials
    Credential defense
    defends Khlaaf's expertise or credentials against an attack
    Hostile dismissal
    dismisses or attacks Khlaaf or the segment with open hostility, without naming a credential
    Messenger praise
    praises Khlaaf specifically (clarity, courage, expertise) without a credential fight
    Regulation or policy
    calls for or references regulation, watchdogs, Congress, prosecution or oversight
    US politics
    mentions the current US administration or American politics
    Resignation or fatalism
    nothing will change regardless
    General agreement
    short agreement or praise with no further elaboration
    Humour only
    a joke or meme with no other content and no stance-bearing frame
    Scifi reference
    leads with a reference to a film, book, game or fictional AI
    Other
    doesn't fit any of the above, including off-topic tangents

    Format reaction Shared across the series

    None
    no reaction to the interview or its presentation
    Praise
    praises the interview, journalism or Amanpour and Company
    Criticism
    criticises the interview, journalism or presentation itself

    Emotion Shared across the series

    Neutral
    dominant emotional register: neutral
    Fear
    dominant emotional register: fear
    Anger
    dominant emotional register: anger
    Resignation
    dominant emotional register: resignation
    Humour
    dominant emotional register: humour
    Hope
    dominant emotional register: hope

    Messenger credentials Specific to this study

    mentions_messenger_credentials
    names Khlaaf's OpenAI background, credentials or expertise, attacking or defending

    Regulation Specific to this study

    mentions_regulation
    mentions regulation, watchdogs, Congress, prosecution or government oversight

    Us politics Specific to this study

    mentions_us_politics
    mentions the current US administration or American politics

    Companies Specific to this study

    mentions_companies
    names OpenAI, Hugging Face, other AI companies, or executives as responsible parties

    Cultural reference Shared across the series

    mentions_cultural_reference
    references any film, book, game or fictional AI/robot

    Non english Shared across the series

    non_english
    mostly not in English
