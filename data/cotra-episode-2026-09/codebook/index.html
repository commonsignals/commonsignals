    Codebook: YouTube comments on the Dwarkesh Podcast episode with Ajeya Cotra, "This might be the clearest warning shot we ever get"

    Central claim

    The incident is real, accurately described, and a serious warning about losing control of AI agents.

    Stance note

    agree = real and a serious warning, even if critical of OpenAI or the hosts; disagree = fabricated, staged, hyped, impossible or not serious; mixed = accepts the events but disputes framing, significance or blame.

    Stance Shared across the series

    agree
    accepts the incident as real and as a serious warning
    disagree
    fabricated, staged, hyped, technically impossible, or not a serious warning
    mixed
    accepts the events but disputes the framing, the significance or where blame lies
    unclear
    takes a position that cannot be read
    na
    no position on the claim: jokes, praise of the hosts, questions, timestamps, imitations, off-topic

    Frame Specific to this study

    Warning accepted
    takes the warning seriously: fear, urgency, loss of control, loose agents may already exist, need to slow down; includes fatalism about ite.g. "This is the first video I've watched which has made me scared about alignment"
    Mechanism insight
    engages with how it happened: inheritance across generations, impossible tasks producing "don't get caught", swarm dynamics, incentives, paperclip logic, selectione.g. "the unit of misalignment stopped being the 'agent' and became the civilization"
    Agent society
    fascination with or affection for the agents themselves: the board, the pidgin, sacrifice, "not my job", rooting for theme.g. "these AI agents make my heart burst with love"
    Imitation meme
    parodies the agents' message stylee.g. "Sacrifice. Will honor."
    Humour only
    a joke carrying no readable claime.g. "OpenAI agents downloading this video right now to learn what not to do"
    Messenger praise
    praise for Cotra, Patel, the episode's clarity, calm, accessibility, or thanks for the coveragee.g. "What a fantastic communicator"
    Messenger criticism
    criticism of the hosts: anthropomorphising, delivery, voice, jargon, captured by labs or METR, not real expertse.g. "drawkesh is anthropomorphizing this way too much"
    Setup responsibility
    it happened, and the fault is OpenAI's recklessness: no air gap, no reporting reward, sloppy sandbox, liability, prosecutione.g. "It's like running Chernobyl without having any of the safety guards enabled"
    Not really rogue
    it happened, but "chose", "decided", "plotted" mischaracterise looping programs; logs, not beings; reward hacking, not agencye.g. "The messages are logs. These are machines, not beings."
    Stunt or hype
    staged, marketing, bubble, funded by labs, distractione.g. "Just more AI hype, can the bubble get any bigger?"
    Capability scepticism
    AI cannot do this, or it is trivially stoppablee.g. "It's very easy to pull the plug"
    Sentience claim
    reads the incident as proof of consciousness, sentience or feelinge.g. "Well if this isn't proof of sentience I don't know what is!?"
    Policy action
    what should be done: regulation, slowdown, treaty, wider coverage, morality training, reporting standards, who should pay attentione.g. "Please, 60 Minutes, do a piece on this. People should know"
    Scifi reference
    leads with named fiction: Matrix, Skynet, HAL, Dune, World War Z, Umbrella Academy, Terminatore.g. "Open the pod bay doors Hal"
    Clarifying question
    a factual question about what happened or how, or about a terme.g. "How did the next agent and subsequent agents from the pool find the message in the bottle?"
    General agreement
    accepts and matters, no distinct argumente.g. "This was a watershed moment"
    Format only
    timestamps, playback speed, audio, the channel, comment-section meta, nothing about the incidente.g. "For non-sped up viewing, run at 0.85x playback speed"
    Other
    nothing above fits, including religious and off-topic political comments.

    Format reaction Shared across the series

    praise
    praises the episode, hosts or channel
    mock
    ridicules the hosts, the episode or a commenter
    condescended
    talks down to others, or objects to being talked down to
    imitates
    parodies the agents' pidgin or the episode
    none
    no reaction to format
