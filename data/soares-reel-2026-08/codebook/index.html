    Codebook: Instagram comments on Nate Soares's reel "We stop AI. You help?"

    Central claim

    AI poses a serious existential danger to humanity, and this is worth taking seriously and acting on.

    Stance note

    agree = accepts AI is a serious existential danger worth acting on; disagree = rejects it as incapable, hype or fearmongering; mixed = accepts part, rejects or reframes part (e.g. blames companies, not AI).

    Stance Shared across the series

    Agree
    accepts AI is a serious existential danger worth acting on
    Disagree
    rejects the claim: not capable, hype, fearmongering
    Mixed
    accepts part, rejects or reframes part
    Na
    on topic but no readable position
    Unclear
    genuinely cannot be told either way; used sparingly

    Frame Specific to this study

    Warning accepted
    straightforwardly agrees AI is dangerous and this is a real warning
    Capability scepticism
    AI isn't actually that smart or capable; doubts the premise on technical grounds
    Stunt or hype
    dismisses the video, incident or field as marketing, fearmongering or hype without a specific capability argument
    Accountability frame
    the real danger is the people, companies or billionaires controlling AI, not the AI itself
    Not really programmed
    technical pushback that AI is just software or code, not sentient, only does what it's told
    Imitation meme
    writes in the video's own simplified caveman register as a joke or homage, no other stated position
    Format criticism
    criticises the simplified delivery as condescending, dumbed-down or patronising
    Format praise
    praises the simplified delivery as helpful, clear or well-explained
    Scifi reference
    leads with a reference to a film, book, game or fictional AI
    Direct action
    calls for direct or physical action against AI or its infrastructure
    Policy regulation
    calls for legislative, regulatory or political action
    Resignation fatalism
    nothing can be done, it's already too late, doomed regardless
    Near term harm
    centres a present-day harm (water, energy, jobs) rather than the existential claim
    Sentience question
    asks or argues about whether AI is conscious, sentient or deserves moral consideration
    Messenger credibility
    comments on Soares, MIRI, the book, or his credibility specifically
    General agreement
    short agreement or praise with no further elaboration
    Humour only
    a joke, meme or reaction with no other content and no stance-bearing frame
    Other
    doesn't fit any of the above; off-topic tangents

    Format reaction Shared across the series

    None
    no reaction to the simplified-English format
    Imitates
    writes in the same simplified register
    Mock
    mocks or ridicules the format
    Condescended
    treats the format as insulting or patronising
    Praise
    praises the format as effective or helpful
