Featured research

"A problem they created": how a news audience received the accountability version of the OpenAI and Hugging Face incident

We coded 322 YouTube comments on Amanpour and Company's interview with Heidy Khlaaf, who argues the "rogue AI" story is the wrong one. Most of the audience agreed with her, got angrier as the thread aged, and asked for regulation without being prompted.

Read more

"Please honor commit": how the OpenAI agent-swarm story landed with the audience most likely to believe it

We coded 1,245 YouTube comments on the Dwarkesh Podcast's interview with METR's Ajeya Cotra about the OpenAI agent-swarm incident. The audience accepted the danger, joked about it, praised the messenger, and had no idea what to do next.

Read more

OpenAI and shut? What an independent report did to the marketing-stunt argument

We compared Reddit comments on the OpenAI and Hugging Face incident before and after METR and Redwood Research published an independent investigation. Afterwards, fewer people called it a stunt and more accepted it happened. The ones still calling it a stunt got harder to shift.

Read more

How Instagram received Nate Soares's caveman-speak explainer of AI risk

We coded 1,534 Instagram comments on a one-minute reel by Nate Soares of the Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI). Most commenters accepted the argument. Many said it was already too late, and almost nobody asked what to do.

Why Common Signals

Global AI investment reached around $581.7bn in 2025 [1]. A sliver of that goes to safety. A sliver of the sliver goes to helping the public understand any of it.

$581.7bnglobal AI investment, 2025

Grounded in evidence

The people trying to explain AI mostly work from instinct. Early evidence, including the Seismic Foundation's 2025 study [2], suggests the field's dominant framings underperform.

Neutral by design

No policy position, no campaigns, no proprietary advice. Findings go to every side of the AI conversation, which is what lets one body credibly serve both.

A proven model, applied early

Built on proven methods and applied to AI while the gap is still wide open.
