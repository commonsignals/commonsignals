Frequently asked questions

What happens in a workshop?

You bring your own materials and your target audience. We review the materials against the tested findings, work through the message choices for that audience, and you leave with specific changes to make rather than a slide deck.

Who should attend?

A small group from one organisation: the people who write and sign off the messaging, and ideally the spokesperson who delivers it. Sessions are built around your materials, so they work best when the people in the room can act on what comes out.

Are workshops in person or remote?

Either. Format and length are set by what your organisation needs.

When can we book one?

Workshops run once the evidence exists to run them on, so they follow the pilot findings. Email hello@commonsignals.org to be first in line.

What do they cost?

Workshops and message reviews are a small earned-income line for Common Signals, planned for later in the roadmap, so they will be priced. Two rules apply: the methods and findings from any paid work can be published, and nothing learned in a paid session is withheld from the free toolkit.

Will you write our messaging for us?

No. We will tell you what the evidence says about your audience and your materials, and where the evidence runs out. The writing, and the position, stay yours.
