    Work with us

    Common Signals builds a shared, neutral evidence base for anyone explaining AI to the public.

    We're at an early stage, with published reception studies, an open methodology, a public data explorer and a map of trusted messengers.

    If you recognise yourself in one of these sections, we'd like to hear from you.

    Fund the work

    So far our research has used data that already exists: public comments on videos, podcasts and posts about AI risk. That work is cheap and it has produced useful hypotheses, but it can only tell us about people who chose to comment.

    The next step is research we own. We're preparing a UK survey, followed by the US, that tests messages with samples recruited by values rather than demographics, so we can see what lands with people who haven't made up their minds about AI. Funding covers fieldwork, open publication of the data and the time to run it properly.

    Everything we produce is published openly under a Creative Commons licence. A grant to Common Signals becomes a resource for every organisation communicating about AI, not an asset for one campaign. We'd rather talk to you while the research design is still open than after it's fixed. Our funder brief is available on request.

    Collaborate on research

    Public attitudes to AI, message testing, audience segmentation, content analysis of AI media: if your question overlaps with ours, we would rather work on it together than in parallel. Our coded datasets are in the data explorer under CC BY 4.0, so you can start from them today.

    We also need methodology review. Our methodology page sets out how we sample comments, code them and turn patterns into hypotheses. We'd like people with survey, content analysis or audience research experience to pick holes in it, and a small group of reviewers willing to read our drafts before publication.

    And we're working out what Common Signals should become: a small in-house team, a network of affiliated researchers or a programme inside an existing institution. If you've worked in or around organisations like Climate Outreach or the FrameWorks Institute, your view on what size and shape works would save us from expensive mistakes.

    Propose a collaboration →

    Shape how findings are used

    Research only matters if it changes what people write, film and say. We're looking for communicators to help with two things.

    The first is editorial: shaping findings into formats practitioners actually use, such as briefings, message guides and before-and-after examples. This could mean reviewing drafts or writing with us.

    The second is assessment criteria. Engagement numbers are a poor proxy for whether a message worked, since a post can go viral because people are angry at it. We want to agree shared criteria with practitioners: did the message change understanding, did it build or erode trust, did it backfire with a particular segment. If you've had to judge your own campaigns and found the metrics wanting, we'd value your input.

    Offer editorial help →

    Help us track messages over time

    Brand trackers measure how awareness and perception of a brand shift month by month across a defined audience. Nobody does this for public understanding of AI.

    We want to track which messages and frames people have encountered, which they remember and how their views move over time, broken down by values segment. We're looking for a survey, panel or tracking platform willing to partner on this, in kind or at cost. In return the tracker would be public, cited and built on a methodology open to scrutiny.

    Talk to us about tracking →

    Make content about AI

    If you make videos, podcasts or posts about AI, your audience's reactions are some of the best evidence available. We can run a reception study on one of your pieces and share the findings with you first, help you test variations of a message with different audiences, and list you on the messenger map so organisations can find trusted voices outside their usual circles. We only publish analysis of your content with your agreement.

    Suggest a piece to analyse →

    Bring us into a cohort

    If you run a fellowship, incubator, accelerator or creator platform for people communicating about AI, we can bring the research into your programme through workshops and training built on our findings. We're also interested in working with cohorts as a test bed: participants try messages based on our hypotheses, we measure what happens, and everyone gets the results. Experienced researchers and communicators can also mentor a project.
