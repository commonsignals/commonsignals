    “Neither company is acting responsibly”: How Jacob Coxon's resignation from Anthropic was received on X

    We coded 2,480 X replies to Jacob Coxon's Anthropic resignation thread, and found the most evenly contested audience in this series so far — one where the single most-liked reply took no position on the argument at all.

    Read the analysis →

  • 14 September 2026

    'A problem they created': how did the accountability version of the OpenAI / Hugging Face incident land with a news audience?

    We coded all 283 YouTube comments on Amanpour and Company's interview with Heidy Khlaaf, who argues the "rogue AI" story is the wrong one, and found a mainstream audience that already agreed with her, arrived angry, and asked for regulation without being prompted.

    Read the analysis →

    Creating a social mandate for AI governance

    Common Signals' theory of change: why public engagement is critical to durable AI governance, and how a social mandate for it gets built.

  • 9 September 2026

    The clearest warning shot: how did the OpenAI agent-swarm story land with the audience most likely to believe it?

    We coded 1,245 YouTube comments on the Dwarkesh Podcast's interview with METR's Ajeya Cotra about the OpenAI agent-swarm incident, and found an audience that accepts the danger, jokes about it at scale, praises the messenger, and still has nowhere to put its fear.

    Read the analysis →

  • 4 September 2026

    OpenAI and Shut: how METR's investigation of the OpenAI and Hugging Face incident closed the marketing-stunt argument

    In July, a Reddit thread about OpenAI's rogue agents was full of people calling it a marketing stunt. After METR and Redwood Research published an independent investigation, we coded 692 comments across the same ecosystem and found the stunt frame had collapsed from a quarter of all comments to one in twenty.

    Read the analysis →

Frequently asked questions

What gets posted here?

Pilot progress, methods and findings, as they are ready. New studies appear here on the day they go live, along with methodological changes and notes on what we got wrong.

How often do you publish?

Studies go up when they are done, which has meant six posts between 28 August and 18 September 2026. The email newsletter goes out roughly monthly.

How do I get updates by email?

Sign up with the form on this page. Roughly monthly, no more than that, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Where else can I follow the work?

Substack (commonsignalsorg.substack.com), LinkedIn and YouTube. Research summaries are also posted to the EA Forum.
