# Findings

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Our findings are now kept as the eleven hypotheses in the Common Signals research agenda. This page lists the six studies behind them and points each earlier finding to its hypothesis.

From 11 November 2026, the patterns this page used to list are maintained as the eleven hypotheses in the [Common Signals research agenda](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda), which says how often each has been seen, which four to settle first and how each could be tested. H1 to H9 keep this page's numbering. H10 and H11 come from the Diary of a CEO study.

Each study codes the full comment thread under one piece of AI communication. None is a claim about what the public thinks: the studies generate hypotheses, and the agenda is where those hypotheses are kept.

## Where each finding went

Links to the sections this page used to have still land here, and each points on to its place in the agenda.

- **1. Acceptance is high everywhere.** Now [H1. Audiences that engage with AI risk content mostly accept the risk already](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h1).
- **2. Persuasion does not produce action.** Now [H2. Persuaded audiences do not ask what to do](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h2).
- **3. Threat without efficacy produced resignation.** Now [H3. The emotion a telling produces depends on what it supplies](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h3).
- **4. A concrete incident removes the capability objection.** Now [H4. A documented incident removes the capability objection, and forecasts bring it back](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h4).
- **5. Once capability is conceded, disagreement is about motive and messenger.** Now [H5. Once capability is conceded, disagreement is about motive and messenger](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h5).
- **6. Independent verification moves sceptics.** Now [H6. Independent verification moves sceptics when the facts stay the same](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h6).
- **7. A named, calm, trusted messenger is rewarded.** Now [H7. A named, calm, credentialed messenger is rewarded, and the credential is attacked first](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h7).
- **8. Accountability is the strongest bridging-frame hypothesis.** Now [H8. Blaming the people and companies in charge is a frame believers and sceptics share](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h8).
- **9. Humour aids distribution.** Now [H9. Humour spreads a story without lowering agreement, and does not carry its conclusion](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#h9).
- **What is new.** The measurements it listed are now the evidence under each hypothesis, [H1 to H11](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#the-hypotheses).
- **What we would test next.** Now [Priorities for 2027](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#priorities-for-2027) and [How to test each hypothesis](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#how-to-test-each-hypothesis).
- **Method and limits.** Now the [methodology page](https://commonsignals.org/methodology) and the agenda's [standard a study must meet](https://commonsignals.org/research/agenda#the-standard-a-study-must-meet).
