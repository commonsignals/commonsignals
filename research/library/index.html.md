# Research library.

Existing research on public attitudes to AI, and on how to frame and communicate AI risk, across both the existential-risk and present-day-harms conversations. New to this? Start with our [reading list](https://commonsignals.org/ai-safety-comms-reading-list).

Listing here is not an endorsement. These summaries describe what each study did and found; Common Signals does not rate, rank or assess them. We list research from both the existential-risk and present-day-harms conversations, on purpose.

## Public attitudes: polls and trackers

Polls, trackers and interview studies of what people think about AI.

### [AI Public Opinion Tracker wave 4](https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/cic/docs/pdfs/wave4_ai_public_opinion_tracker.pdf)

University of South Carolina College of Information and Communications · 2026 · US adults · fieldwork Winter 2025 to 2026

tracker · US · tracker

Fourth wave of a repeated US tracker on public attitudes to AI, published as a findings report.

[Report](https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/cic/docs/pdfs/wave4_ai_public_opinion_tracker.pdf) · [PDF](https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/cic/docs/pdfs/wave4_ai_public_opinion_tracker.pdf) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#usc-ai-opinion-tracker-w4)

### [AI Index Report 2026: Public opinion chapter](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/public-opinion)

Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI · 2026 · Synthesis of Ipsos and Pew and other global polling · fieldwork 2025

review · Global · report

The AI Index's annual public opinion chapter drawing together the major international surveys. Reports the global share seeing more benefits than drawbacks from AI rose from 55% in 2024 to 59% in 2025 while the share saying AI makes them nervous rose to 52%.

**Key finding:** 59% globally see more benefits than drawbacks, up from 55% in 2024

[Report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/public-opinion) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#stanford-ai-index-2026-public-opinion)

### [Adults' Media Use and Attitudes 2026](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/media-use-and-attitudes/media-habits-adults/passive-social-media-use-ai-companionship-and-online-side-hustles-uk-adults-media-and-online-lives-revealed)

Ofcom · 2026 · UK adults · fieldwork 2025 to 2026

tracker · UK · report

Ofcom's annual UK media literacy tracker with a growing AI section. Reports 54% of UK adults now use AI tools, up from 31% in 2024, while trust in AI-generated content has not risen alongside use.

**Key finding:** 54% of UK adults use AI tools, up from 31% in 2024

[Report](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/media-use-and-attitudes/media-habits-adults/passive-social-media-use-ai-companionship-and-online-side-hustles-uk-adults-media-and-online-lives-revealed) · [PDF](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/research-and-data/media-literacy-research/adults/adults-media-use-and-attitudes-2026/adults-media-use-and-attitudes-2026-report.pdf?v=415430) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#ofcom-adults-media-2026)

### [Public opinion on AI varies widely by age gender race and frequency of use](https://www.dataforprogress.org/blog/2026/2/27/public-opinion-on-artificial-intelligence-varies-widely-by-age-gender-race-and-frequency-of-use)

Data for Progress · 2026 · US adults · fieldwork 2026

poll · US · poll

Polling analysis of how American attitudes to AI split by demographic group and by frequency of use, with usage emerging as a major dividing line.

[Report](https://www.dataforprogress.org/blog/2026/2/27/public-opinion-on-artificial-intelligence-varies-widely-by-age-gender-race-and-frequency-of-use) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#data-for-progress-ai-2026)

### [What 81,000 people want from AI](https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews)

Anthropic · 2026 · n=80,508 Claude.ai users in 159 countries; not a representative sample · interviews December 2025

interviews · Global · report

AI-led interviews with 80,508 Claude.ai users in 159 countries; existential risk is the least-raised of 13 concerns. An interview study of Claude users, not a representative poll.

[Report](https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews) · [What people worry about](https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews#:~:text=WHAT%20PEOPLE%20WORRY%20ABOUT) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#anthropic-81k-interviews-2026)

### [On the razor's edge: AI vs. everything we care about](https://report2025.seismic.org/)

Seismic Foundation · 2025 · n>10000 across US UK France Germany Poland · fieldwork 2025

segmentation · Multi-country · report

Segmentation study identifying five groups of the public closest to civic action on AI and how AI fits into wider hopes and fears. Reports women twice as likely as men to worry about AI and 60% worried about AI's effect on relationships.

**Key finding:** Women twice as likely as men to worry about AI

[Report](https://report2025.seismic.org/) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#seismic-razors-edge-2025)

### [How Americans view AI and its impact on people and society](https://www.pewresearch.org/science/2025/09/17/how-americans-view-ai-and-its-impact-on-people-and-society/)

Pew Research Center · 2025 · US adults · fieldwork 2025

survey · US · report

Pew's US survey on the increased use of AI in daily life. Reports that 50% of Americans are more concerned than excited, up from 37% in 2021.

**Key finding:** 50% more concerned than excited, up from 37% in 2021

[Report](https://www.pewresearch.org/science/2025/09/17/how-americans-view-ai-and-its-impact-on-people-and-society/) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#pew-us-ai-2025)

### [How people around the world view AI](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2025/10/15/how-people-around-the-world-view-ai/)

Pew Research Center · 2025 · 25 countries · fieldwork 2025

survey · Multi-country · report

Pew's cross-national comparison of concern and excitement about AI, awareness levels and trust in institutions to regulate it, across 25 countries including the UK and Canada.

[Report](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2025/10/15/how-people-around-the-world-view-ai/) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#pew-global-ai-2025)

### [Trust attitudes and use of artificial intelligence: A global study 2025](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/ai-and-technology/trust-attitudes-and-use-of-ai.html)

KPMG and University of Melbourne · 2025 · n>48000 across 47 countries · fieldwork Nov 2024 to Jan 2025

survey · Global · report

The largest global study of public trust in AI. Reports 66% use AI with some regularity while 46% are willing to trust it, and that trust fell and worry rose as adoption grew since the 2022 study.

**Key finding:** 66% use AI regularly, 46% willing to trust it

[Report](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/ai-and-technology/trust-attitudes-and-use-of-ai.html) · [PDF](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/xx/pdf/2025/05/trust-attitudes-and-use-of-ai-global-report.pdf) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#kpmg-melbourne-trust-ai-2025)

Ipsos with the Tony Blair Institute for Global Change · 2025 · UK adults · fieldwork 2025

Joint survey on UK public attitudes to AI, framed around building trust to support adoption. Finds many people in the UK remain cautious about AI.

[Report](https://www.ipsos.com/en-uk/what-uk-thinks-about-ai) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#ipsos-tbi-uk-ai-2025)

### [Flash poll: Trust and artificial intelligence at a crossroads](https://www.edelman.com/trust/2025/trust-barometer/flash-poll-trust-artifical-intelligence)

Edelman Trust Institute · 2025 · n=5000 across 5 countries · fieldwork 17 to 27 Oct 2025

survey · Multi-country · report

Trust Barometer flash poll on trust in AI across five markets. Reports trust in AI at 72% in China against 32% in the US, and that hands-on use is associated with higher trust.

**Key finding:** Trust in AI: 72% in China, 32% in the US

[Report](https://www.edelman.com/trust/2025/trust-barometer/flash-poll-trust-artifical-intelligence) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#edelman-trust-ai-flash-2025)

### [How do people feel about AI? Wave two](https://www.turing.ac.uk/news/publications/understanding-public-attitudes-ai-second-survey)

tracker · UK · tracker

Second wave of the nationally representative UK survey, repeating the 2022 questions to track change, with new material on regulation. A companion data site publishes the tables.

**Key finding:** Nearly nine in ten UK adults support independent regulation of AI

### [Voters prefer AI regulation that mandates safety measures over industry self-regulation](https://theaipi.org/april-voters-prefer-ai-regulation-over-self-regulation/)

poll · US · poll

One of AIPI's recurring US voter polls on AI regulation. Reports around 80% preferring mandated safety standards for the most advanced models over self-regulation, and a 2:1 margin against open-sourcing powerful models.

survey · UK · report

## Framing and risk communication

Research on how framing, messengers and risk perception shape the way people respond to messages about AI.

### [From catastrophic to concrete: Reframing AI risk communication for public mobilization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.06525)

arXiv preprint 2511.06525 · 2025 · Research paper · fieldwork 2025

experiment · US · paper

Communications study on how framing AI risk as concrete and near-term rather than catastrophic and abstract affects public willingness to act.

[Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.06525) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#arxiv-2511-06525-reframing-ai-risk)

### [What's risk got to do with it? Public attitudes toward AI regulation in the European Union and United States](https://doi.org/10.1093/9780198945215.003.0083)

Kulani Abendroth-Dias, Oxford Intersections: AI in Society (Oxford University Press) · 2025 · 65 expert interviews and a survey experiment with 1,310 participants, EU and US

experiment · Multi-country · chapter

Survey experiment in the EU and US: framing AI risk as disinformation or online child safety moved conservatives' support for regulation more than liberals'.

[Chapter](https://doi.org/10.1093/9780198945215.003.0083) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#abendroth-dias-2025-ai-regulation-risk)

### [Temporal framing in balanced news coverage of artificial intelligence and public attitudes](https://doi.org/10.1080/15205436.2023.2248974)

Sukyoung Choi, Mass Communication and Society 27(2), 384-405 · 2023 · Online experiment

experiment · paper

Near-future framing of AI risk.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/15205436.2023.2248974) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#choi-2023-temporal-framing-ai)

### [Risk as analysis and risk as feelings: Some thoughts about affect, reason, risk, and rationality](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0272-4332.2004.00433.x)

Paul Slovic, Melissa L. Finucane, Ellen Peters and Donald G. MacGregor, Risk Analysis 24(2), 311-322 · 2004 · Review article

review · paper

People judge risk by feeling as much as by analysis: a fast, intuitive system driven by affect runs alongside deliberate reasoning, and each depends on the other.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0272-4332.2004.00433.x) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#slovic-2004-risk-as-feelings)

### [Perception of risk](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.3563507)

Paul Slovic, Science 236(4799), 280-285 · 1987 · Research article

review · paper

Foundational review of research on how people judge hazardous activities and technologies, written to help experts and policymakers understand public responses and communicate risk.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.3563507) · [Link to this entry](https://commonsignals.org/research/library/#slovic-1987-perception-of-risk)

## Suggest a study.

We're looking for research that meets all three:

- Research on public attitudes to AI, or on framing and risk communication: primary studies or foundational reviews, not commentary.
- A report, dataset or published paper we can link to directly.
- A verifiable link: the primary source, not press coverage of it.

We don't list opinion pieces, vendor marketing dressed as research, or think-pieces without underlying data.
